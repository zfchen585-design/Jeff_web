About

I am an undergraduate researcher at the University of California, Irvine, pursuing a B.S. in Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering and a minor in Materials Science and Engineering (expected June 2027).

In the Lee Nano-Optics Lab, my conducting-polymer work spans two distinct directions. I fabricate and characterize PEDOT:PSS films for femtosecond-laser processing and third-harmonic generation; that collaboration has produced a manuscript submitted to Nature Materials, on which I am the fourth author. Separately, I study how thermal and ethylene-glycol processing tune the near-infrared epsilon-near-zero (ENZ) response of PEDOT:PSS films, work presented in my 2026 APS poster.

My materials research also includes complex oxide thin films in the Bowman Lab, from phase stability and CO2 electroreduction to Cu-containing films for memristors. During summer 2026, I worked with the Nguyen Lab at UC Santa Barbara on thermal-stability strategies for organic photovoltaics. Across these projects, I combine fabrication, optical and electrical measurements, microscopy, and data analysis.

This site collects selected projects, publications, posters, and instrument experience. Publication statuses are separated below and reflected in the downloadable CV.

Research

Current and recent projects across three research groups.

Lee Nano-Optics Lab · UC Irvine

Femtosecond-laser processing and third-harmonic generation

I fabricate and characterize PEDOT:PSS films to investigate how femtosecond-laser irradiation changes their nonlinear optical response. I measure third-harmonic generation with the Leica SP8 microscope and use ellipsometry to assess film thickness and optical constants. A collaborative manuscript from this work has been submitted to Nature Materials; I am the fourth author.

Lee Nano-Optics Lab · UC Irvine

Near-infrared ENZ response in PEDOT:PSS

This is a separate project on multilayer conducting-polymer films. Thermal and ethylene-glycol processing tune the fitted ENZ wavelength from 971.5 nm in the pristine film to 875.5 nm after 10 minutes of EG treatment. I presented these results at the 2026 APS Far West Section Meeting; a related manuscript is in preparation.

See the APS poster →
Lee Nano-Optics Lab · UC Irvine

Nanoscale transport and mid-infrared materials

Related work includes AFM/conductive-AFM analysis of bias-dependent PEDOT:PSS transport and optical-constant extraction for ITO and ALD-grown AZO thin films.

Bowman Lab · UC Irvine

Complex oxides and memristors

I study composition, deposition conditions, oxygen-vacancy chemistry, and transport in indium-oxide-based complex oxide films. For a CALIT2 UROP IRT project, I helped lead the roadmap for Cu-containing oxide thin films and resistive-switching devices.

See the IRT poster →
Bowman Lab · UC Irvine

Oxide phase stability and electrochemistry

I use solid-state synthesis, XRD phase analysis, EIS, XPS, SEM–EDS, and conductivity measurements to connect processing and structure with functional response in medium- and high-entropy oxides. I also contributed to a published study of glancing-angle-deposited YSZ nanostructures.

Nguyen Lab · UC Santa Barbara · Summer 2026

Thermal stability in organic photovoltaics

I compared crosslinker and thermoset-matrix strategies for stabilizing non-fullerene organic photovoltaic devices, and developed a framework linking network formation to thermal failure and device-function retention.
